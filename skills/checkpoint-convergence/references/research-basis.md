# Research basis and adaptation boundary

This skill generalizes already-researched public engineering patterns rather than copying vendor runtimes.

Shopify Helix informs small ordered checkpoints, reference-as-spec, oracle-before-candidate, ordered gates, repair/re-review, context-isolated review, accepted feedback, policy-bounded autonomy, and checkpoint promotion.

Shopify River informs live-state reconciliation and closure verification. ShopGym informs fresh execution contexts built from current code/spec/feedback. Roast informs explicit structured workflows and moving deterministic guarantees from prompts into code.

These sources are evidence inputs only. RhapsodIA does not depend on Shopify models, mobile architecture, proprietary tools, or exact gate taxonomy.
