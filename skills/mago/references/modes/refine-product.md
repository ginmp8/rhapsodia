# Refine Product Mode

## Canonical Rules

- Resolve one canonical registry-backed package.
- Preserve immutable identity and existing truthful planning history.
- Touch only `prd.md`, `notes.md`, and optional product-oriented `validation.md`.

## Product Refinement Workflow

1. Read current product documents first and preserve what remains true.
2. Load only evidence needed to resolve current ambiguity or contradiction.
3. Tighten scope, decisions, assumptions, risks, trade-offs, questions, and conceptual validation without widening into execution planning.
4. Use local normalization/validation scripts when structural reconciliation is needed.
5. Stop when documents are clearer and internally consistent.

## Boundaries

- do not create or alter tasks, registry identity/dependencies/handoff, manifest execution fields, generated views, or execution evidence;
- do not decompose work, infer implementation progress, or backfill rollout state;
- leave task/execution sections unchanged and route mixed legacy content through `adapt`;
- when product and task contracts cannot be reconciled without touching both, switch to full `refine` rather than crossing mode boundaries.

## Refinement Focus

Preserve the initiative boundary, improve decision clarity, and keep changes minimal when the package is already coherent.
