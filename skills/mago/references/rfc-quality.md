# RFC Quality

Use when prd.md assumptions, options, unresolved decisions, or planning criteria are in scope.

Mago may use RFC-style thinking to improve planning documents, but it does not own Magnomo RFC proposals or approval workflows.

## Planning Quality

- State assumptions explicitly and record confidence or uncertainty when it materially affects planning.
- Define decision criteria before choosing spec boundaries, package shape, validation strategy, or task split.
- Compare at least one rejected option when the choice affects future planning.
- Include status quo or `do nothing now` when it is a real planning option.
- Keep unresolved approval or stakeholder alignment questions in `Open Questions`; do not pretend they are decided.

Do not create governance RFCs from Mago. For material roadmap, priority, stakeholder, ownership, or handoff proposals, use Magnomo `rfc-proposal`.
